I'm Ola,
a Design Engineer.

I got into design in 2020 during the COVID-19 lockdown. I started out as a graphic designer, creating flyers and simple logos for clients. A few months in, I became more interested in the bigger picture of how brands communicate, so I began exploring brand identity design. This led me to work with different clients, helping them build visual identities that were not only attractive, but also memorable and meaningful.

In 2022, my curiosity shifted toward digital products. I found myself wondering how apps worked, how they were built, and how design influenced the way people interacted with them. That curiosity introduced me to product design. I took a few courses, taught myself extensively, and eventually decided to make a full transition into product design.

That same year, I got an internship opportunity at a fintech startup in Nigeria. It was an important turning point in my journey because it gave me the opportunity to apply what I had learned to real products and real users. Since then, I've continued working with founders, startups, and businesses, which has helped me grow as a designer and develop the ability to take ideas from early concepts to polished, thoughtful experiences. I enjoy turning abstract ideas into clear, engaging interfaces and cohesive brand systems.

My approach to design is rooted in understanding people — how they think, interact, and connect with products. I believe good design should go beyond looking good; it should be intuitive, purposeful, and grounded in real user needs. I pay close attention to detail, consistency, and storytelling, making sure every touchpoint contributes to a meaningful experience.

Today, I'm also expanding into design engineering, combining my design foundation with growing technical skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and no-code tools. Learning how products are built allows me to think beyond the interface, design with stronger technical awareness, and collaborate more effectively with developers. I'm interested in bridging the gap between design and development and bringing ideas closer to reality.

Outside of digital design, I'm drawn to fashion and style. I'm fascinated by how people use clothing, culture, and personal style as forms of expression, and that interest naturally influences how I think about aesthetics, composition, and visual identity.

For me, design is an ongoing journey of curiosity, experimentation, and growth. I'm constantly learning, refining my craft, and looking for better ways to turn ideas into experiences that people can genuinely connect with.
